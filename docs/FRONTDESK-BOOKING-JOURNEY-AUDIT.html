The two honest caveats on GROUP

INCONSISTENCY #1 — two screens. You add guests on the groups list page (groups.php), but assign / book / pay live on the group detail page (groups/detail.php), reached via "Manage rooms & rooming". It works, but the add step and the book/pay step are on different screens — easy to think "nothing happened" after adding a guest.

INCONSISTENCY #2 — "pay" = desk payment. "Mark paid" sets the booking to paid with gateway "Front desk" (supplier_groups.php:262-265) — it records that you took payment at the desk. It does not charge the customer online. There is no customer-facing online checkout in the group flow — it's a front-desk / B2B tool. If you expected the group's customer to pay through Paystack/card, that path does not exist here.
